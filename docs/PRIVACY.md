# Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. This policy covers the Nazzel app for Android.

## Summary

Nazzel collects nothing about you. It has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no crash reporting, and it sends no data to its developer or to
any third party for its own purposes.

## What the app does with your data

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Links you paste, share or copy | To find the video you asked for | Sent only to the site the link points to; the list of your downloads stays in a database on the phone |
| Clipboard | When you open the app, a link you copied is filled into the link field | Read on the phone only, never sent anywhere except as a link you then choose to open |
| Downloaded videos and audio | Saved where you ask | `Download/Nazzel`, or the folder you choose |
| Download list, statistics, settings | To show your downloads and defaults | On the phone, removed when you delete an item or uninstall the app |

The app writes no log files. It asks for no location, contacts, camera,
microphone, photos or account permission.

## Network connections

The app connects to the site of each link you open, to fetch the video and its
preview image. Those sites can see your IP address and the request, as with any
browser. The Google Play build makes no other connection. The version published
on GitHub also asks GitHub and the yt-dlp project once a day for newer versions
of its downloader and of the app, and downloads them.

## Permissions

- Internet, to download
- Notifications, to show progress and completion
- Foreground service (data sync), to keep a download running while the app is closed

## Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Your content

You are responsible for downloading only content you have the right to save.
The app does not bypass paid access or copy protection.
